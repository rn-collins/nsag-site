Intelligence Layer

NSAG Intelligence Products

Four products built from the framework. Each base subscription is a document that arrives — produced, reviewed, and delivered monthly or quarterly. No discovery call or advisory relationship required at the subscription tier. Add Q&A Access to any module-tier subscription for $200/mo (written Q&A only, no call). Add Analyst Access to any product for $500/mo and get a 30-minute monthly call plus unlimited written Q&A between deliveries. All four products also offer a Module Intelligence tier — buy Compass or Brief for a single module at a lower entry price, with a natural upgrade to the full base at three or more modules.

NSAG Compass

Regulatory & Legislative Monitoring

What you receive each month

A PDF report covering regulatory developments, federal and state legislation, court decisions, and agency guidance — each mapped to the specific NSAG module and governance dimension it affects. Arrives automatically. No discovery call or advisory relationship required at the subscription tier.

Base covers all 15 modules. Sector depth add-ons increase coverage in a specific sector: Legal Pack (M1/M2/M6), Healthcare Pack (M3/M4/M8/M9/M10/M11/M13), or Civic Pack (M5/M7/M12). Add Analyst Access to ask follow-up questions and get a monthly call on the findings.

Pricing

Module tier: $150/mo per module  ·  Full base: $350/mo (all 15 modules — more cost-effective at 3+)
+$200/mo per sector depth pack  ·  +$500/mo Analyst Access  ·  $500/mo bundled with Brief

NSAG Brief

Research Evidence Synthesis

What you receive each month

A PDF synthesizing new peer-reviewed research across all 15 module domains — written for governance practitioners in practical terms. Each study is mapped to specific assessment dimensions with practitioner-level implications stated plainly. Arrives automatically.

Base issue covers all 15 modules. Module deep-dive add-ons deliver a full literature review, annotated bibliography, and governance implications memo for one specific module. Add Analyst Access for monthly Q&A on the research.

Pricing

Module tier: $100/mo per module  ·  Full base: $250/mo (all 15 modules — more cost-effective at 3+)
+$150/mo per module deep-dive add-on  ·  +$500/mo Analyst Access  ·  $500/mo bundled with Compass
