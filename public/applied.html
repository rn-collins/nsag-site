NSAG APPLIED

The Framework in Sector-Specific Practice

The 16 principles of Nervous-System-Aware Governance adapt to specific sectors, populations, and institutional contexts. These five applied brands deploy the NSAG framework where the stakes are highest. Some serve institutions; others serve individuals directly — a woman researching her own care, a parent protecting a child’s developing brain. NSAG scales from the single person to the whole system.
