M1–M15 assessment status

All fifteen module pages and their legacy assessment deployments are reachable. They remain educational prototypes—not validated instruments, audits, accreditations, certifications, institutional findings, or substitutes for professional review.

Current release boundary: NSAG is pausing email-gated assessment submissions, server-generated reports, newsletter enrollment, and automated follow-up while the shared instrument, consent, retention, delivery, and accessibility contracts are rebuilt. No assessment response should be submitted to NSAG at this stage.

Advisory discussion

Questions about the framework or a possible engagement are reviewed by RN. There is no automatic recommendation, certification, implementation, or report delivery.

What is not active

Email report delivery

No claim is made that entering an email sends a report. The supported public result, where available, is a browser-generated educational artifact.

Benchmarking and validation

No representative cohort, psychometric validation, sector benchmark, audit opinion, or organizational certification is currently offered.

Automated intelligence

No scheduled report, subscription, dashboard, or automated analyst service is active on this deployment.

Judgment boundary

One respondent’s answers cannot establish an institution’s actual policies, performance, legal compliance, safety, equity, or outcomes. Any future organizational assessment requires source review, stakeholder participation, scope definition, human analysis, and an explicit limitations record.
