Privacy notice

Minimal collection while prototypes are paused.

Last updated 15 August 2026.

Assessment data

The supported experience does not accept assessment answers, scores, report requests, or newsletter enrollment. Legacy prototype interactions may calculate locally in your browser; do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

Basic traffic data

The site may send a cookieless page-view event containing the page path and time to NSAG’s service. It is used for aggregate operational visibility and is not intended to identify you.

Direct contact

Email links open your mail provider. If you choose to write, NSAG receives the information you include. Contact inquiries require an explicit purpose and are intended to be retained for no more than 90 days in the supported service.

Inactive services

Automated reports, newsletters, digest delivery, and the Data Rights Unit collection workflow are paused. No payment checkout is active.

Your choices

You can browse without submitting contact information. For access, correction, or deletion questions, email contact@nsag.org. Do not send sensitive records by ordinary email.
