What NSAG Brief Is

Governance intelligence. Monthly.

NSAG Brief synthesizes peer-reviewed research across all 15 modules of the Neurocognitive Systems Advisory Group framework. Each entry identifies the primary source for the module domain and, where applicable, an additional supporting source. Citations are in APA format.

Every claim is attributed to a primary peer-reviewed source unless otherwise noted. Secondary sources are labeled explicitly. The governance implication follows each summary: what this evidence means for how your institution is designed, and what governance failure looks like when this evidence is not applied.

This is a practitioner resource. You do not need a title to use it: a patient, a parent, a board member, or a citizen can read a single entry and speak to the evidence in the rooms where these decisions are made. The goal is that the person reading a Brief entry on a Tuesday morning can bring it to a governance conversation on Wednesday and speak to the evidence without having read the original paper.

Each Issue Contains

  • 13 module entries — covering 13 of the 15 module domains
  • Primary source citation in APA per module
  • Supporting source where relevant
  • Practitioner summary: what the study found and why it matters
  • Governance implication: what this means for your institutional design
