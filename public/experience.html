Delivery model

Human-supervised from scope to recommendation.

NSAG’s available work is advisory. A person confirms the question, evidence boundary, limitations, and deliverable before work begins.

There is no instant tier report, automated analyst, guaranteed benchmark, or self-serve paid engagement. Module assessments remain prelaunch prototypes.

1. Scope

We clarify the decision, stakeholders, jurisdiction, evidence, and exclusions.

2. Review

A human reviews the available material and states assumptions and uncertainty.

3. Deliver

Any timeline, price, format, and follow-up are agreed directly before commitment.
