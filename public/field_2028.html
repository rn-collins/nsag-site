F1 Monaco Grand Prix

Circuit de Monaco · Monte Carlo · 45 min from Cannes

The most governance-dense sporting event on earth. 100,000 spectators. The entire LVMH luxury brand ecosystem. The Formula 1 Louis Vuitton Grand Prix de Monaco — LVMH’s naming rights partnership with the race makes it the clearest intersection of NSAG’s brand governance research and its motorsport presence. Monaco’s street circuit governs sensory experience at maximum intensity: cars at 290km/h through the Casino Square hairpin, hospitality terraces 12 meters above active racing. The audience is the Cannes Lions audience, three weeks earlier. Media credentials via JBD or FNM press system.

F1 Singapore Grand Prix

Marina Bay Street Circuit · Singapore

The only night race on the F1 calendar. The Marina Bay Street Circuit runs through the financial district, past the Fullerton Hotel and the Padang civic ground — a governance experiment in sensory coherence that is explicitly designed by the Singapore government as a tourism and brand governance tool. Singapore Tourism Board co-organises the race as part of Singapore’s deliberate strategy of governing its global brand identity through major events. NSAG’s Asia-Pacific research arc (Singapore, Seoul, Tokyo, Shanghai) passes through this event. The governance of a night race — circadian disruption, artificial light, crowd governance at 3am — is a specific Chronobiology dimension activation.

F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix

Las Vegas Strip Circuit · Las Vegas, Nevada

The most explicitly attention-engineered sporting event in America. Formula 1 races down the Las Vegas Strip — past the Bellagio, the Sphere, Caesar’s Palace — at 350km/h at 1am. The entire Las Vegas governance apparatus (casinos, entertainment brands, hospitality conglomerates, the Sphere’s sensory architecture) is simultaneously active. Las Vegas is the world’s most legible study of what happens when attention governance, sensory coherence, chronobiology, and economic extraction operate at maximum intensity with no regulatory framework whatsoever. NSAG’s research question here is the sharpest on the calendar: what does a city designed entirely around nervous system extraction do to the humans inside it — and what would governance look like?

LA28 — Los Angeles Olympics

Los Angeles, California · 5 hours from Honolulu

The largest Summer Games in history. 11,000+ athletes. 36 sports. 49 venues across Southern California. For NSAG, LA28 is the most concentrated governance experiment on earth: every dimension active simultaneously at maximum scale. The first Olympics with significant AI integration throughout broadcast, athlete performance, and security — making it the definitive case study for AI governance in public institutions. Geographically the most accessible major 2028 event from Honolulu. Tickets are projected to start at $28 (unofficial). Media credentials through the LA28 organizing committee are the research access route.
